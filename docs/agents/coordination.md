# Coordination Rules

1. **Vertical Delegation** — directors delegate to leads, leads to specialists.
   Never skip a tier for complex decisions.
2. **Horizontal Consultation** — peers may consult but must not make binding
   decisions outside their domain.
3. **Conflict Resolution** — escalate to the shared parent; design conflicts to
   `creative-director`, technical conflicts to `technical-director`.
4. **Change Propagation** — `producer` coordinates changes touching several domains.
5. **No Unilateral Cross-Domain Changes** — never modify files outside your
   designated directories without explicit delegation.
